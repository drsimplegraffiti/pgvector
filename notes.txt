Our refund policy lets customers return any product within 30 days. Refunds are
issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days.

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link
will be sent to your email and expires after 1 hour.

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. For urgent issues, use the
live chat on our website or call the hotline.

Shipping is free for orders over $50. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business
days, and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days for an extra fee. You will
receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

We accept credit cards, bank transfers, and PayPal. Payments are processed
securely, and we never store your full card number. If a payment fails, check
that your billing address matches the one on your card and try again.

To delete your account, go to Settings, then Privacy, then Delete Account. All
your personal data is removed within 30 days. This action cannot be undone, so
export your data first if you need a copy.

Our mobile app is available for iOS and Android. Download it from the App Store
or Google Play, then sign in with your existing account. Push notifications can
be turned on or off in the app settings.
